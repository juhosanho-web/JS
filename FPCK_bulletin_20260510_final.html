Psalm 126 — The Lord Restores His People
"When the Lord restored the fortunes of Zion, we were like those who dream." — Ps. 126:1

God's restoration is beyond imagination — like dreamers who cannot believe their joy. As we sow in tears, He promises we shall reap with songs of joy. Let us bring our sorrows to Him, trusting in His faithful redemption.
