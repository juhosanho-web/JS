Study Notes

This psalm is David's cry against the injustice of the wicked, expressing his firm confidence in God's righteous judgment.
NIV uses be glad instead of ESV's rejoice, and avenged instead of vengeance, giving it a more conversational tone.
"Surely" is repeated twice to emphasize David's unwavering conviction in God's justice, and "still are rewarded" affirms that God's reward is present and ongoing.
